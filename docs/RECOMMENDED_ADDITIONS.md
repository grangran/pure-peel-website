# Recommended Additions to Pure Peel Co. Website

## 🎯 Top Priority (Highest Impact on Sales)

### 1. **Customer Reviews & Ratings** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Builds trust, increases conversions by 15-30%, improves SEO

**What to add:**
- Review section on each product page
- Star ratings (1-5 stars)
- Customer photos with reviews
- Review request email after order delivery
- Verified purchase badges

**Implementation:**
- Add review form to order confirmation/tracking page
- Store reviews in database (or use service like Trustpilot, Yotpo)
- Display on product pages with filtering/sorting

**Impact:** **HIGH** - Social proof is critical for e-commerce

---

### 2. **Email Newsletter Signup** ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Direct marketing channel, repeat customers, abandoned cart recovery

**What to add:**
- Newsletter signup in footer
- Exit-intent popup with discount offer (e.g., "Get 10% off!")
- Welcome email with discount code
- Abandoned cart emails
- Product recommendations via email

**Implementation:**
- Add signup form (integrate with Resend or Mailchimp)
- Create email templates
- Set up automated sequences

**Impact:** **HIGH** - Builds customer list for marketing

---

### 3. **Product Recommendations** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Increases average order value by 20-30%

**What to add:**
- "Frequently bought together" section
- "You may also like" on product pages
- "Recently viewed" products
- Bundle deals (e.g., "Buy 3, Get 10% off")

**Implementation:**
- Track product views in localStorage
- Suggest complementary products
- Create product bundles

**Impact:** **HIGH** - Customers buy more items per order

### 4. **FAQ Section** ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Why:** Reduces support requests, answers common questions, improves SEO

**What to add:**
- FAQ page with common questions
- FAQ section on product pages
- Searchable FAQ
- Categories (Shipping, Products, Orders, Returns)

**Common Questions:**
- How long do products last?
- How to store dehydrated citrus?
- What's the difference between sizes?
- Can I use these in cocktails?
- Do you ship to the US?

**Impact:** **MEDIUM-HIGH** - Reduces support load, builds trust

### 5. **Live Chat / Support Widget** ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Implementation:**
- Add free chat widget (Tawk.to is free)

**Impact:** **MEDIUM-HIGH** - More sales, better customer service
