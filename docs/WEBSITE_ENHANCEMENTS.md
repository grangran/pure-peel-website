# Website Enhancement Ideas for Pure Peel Co.

## High Priority (Quick Wins - High Impact)

### 1. Customer Reviews & Ratings ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Impact:** Builds trust, increases conversions, improves SEO

**What to add:**
- Product review section on each product page
- Star ratings display
- Customer photos with reviews
- Review submission after order completion

**Implementation:**
- Add review form to order confirmation page
- Store reviews in database or use a service (Trustpilot, Yotpo)
- Display reviews on product pages

**Result:** More social proof = more sales
