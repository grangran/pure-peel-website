# Shipping Times by Service - Complete Breakdown

## Order Processing

### Order Cutoff Time
- **Time:** 2:00 PM EST (Eastern Standard Time, Toronto)
- **Timezone:** (GMT-05:00) Eastern Standard Time

### Handling Time
- **Min:** 0 days (if order placed before 2:00 PM EST)
- **Max:** 1 day (if order placed after 2:00 PM EST or on weekends)
- **Fulfilled:** Monday - Saturday
- **Note:** Orders placed Friday-Sunday are processed the following Monday

## Shipping Services - Canada

**Order Cutoff:**
- 2:00 PM EST (Toronto)

**Handling Time:**
- Min: 0 days
- Max: 1 day
- Fulfilled: Mon – Sat

**Transit Time:**
- Min: 2 days
- Max: 5 days
- Shipped: Mon – Fri

**Total Delivery Time:**
- **2-6 business days** (handling 0-1 + transit 2-5)

**Example:**
- Order placed Monday 10:00 AM → Processed Monday → Shipped Monday/Tuesday → Delivered Wednesday-Friday
- Order placed Monday 3:00 PM → Processed Tuesday → Shipped Tuesday/Wednesday → Delivered Thursday-Monday

## Important Notes

### Handling Time Details

**0 days handling:**
- Order placed **before 2:00 PM EST** on Monday-Thursday
- Processed same day
- Shipped same day or next morning

**1 day handling:**
- Order placed **after 2:00 PM EST** on any day
- Order placed on **Friday-Sunday**
- Processed next business day
- Shipped next business day

### Transit Time Details

**Transit time starts when:**
- Package is handed off to carrier (Canada Post)
- Not when order is placed
- Not when order is processed

**Business days:**
- Monday - Friday (excluding holidays)
- Saturday and Sunday don't count
- Holidays don't count

### Remote Areas

**Yukon, Northwest Territories, Nunavut:**
- Same transit times as standard areas
- But may experience additional delays
- 25% shipping cost surcharge applies
