# How to Get More Users to Your Website - Simple Action Plan

## The 3 Most Important Things (Do These First!)

### 1. Google Search Console (30 minutes) ⭐ MOST IMPORTANT

**This is how Google finds and shows your website in search results.**

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console
2. Sign in with your Google account
3. Click "Add Property"
4. Enter: `https://purepeelco.com` (without www)
5. Choose "URL prefix" method
6. Verify ownership (Google will give you options):
   - **Easiest:** Download HTML file → Upload to `public/google-xxxxx.html` → Verify
   - **Or:** Add meta tag to `index.html` → Verify
7. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" → Enter `sitemap.xml` → Submit

**Result:** Google will start indexing your site within 24-48 hours!

---

### 2. Set Up Google Analytics (15 minutes)

**This shows you who visits your site and where they come from.**

**Steps:**
1. Go to: https://analytics.google.com
2. Create account (if you don't have one)
3. Set up property:
   - Name: "Pure Peel Co."
   - Website: `https://purepeelco.com`
   - Industry: Food & Beverage
4. Get your Measurement ID (looks like `G-XXXXXXXXXX`)
5. Add to your website (we can help with this)

**Result:** You'll see how many people visit, which pages they view, and where they come from.

---

### 3. Share on Social Media (Ongoing)

**Your Instagram already has followers - drive them to your website!**

**What to do:**
1. **Update Instagram Bio:**
   - Add link: `https://purepeelco.com`
   - Use link in bio tool (Linktree, etc.) if you want multiple links

2. **Post About Your Website:**
   - "Shop now at purepeelco.com!"
   - "New products available on our website"
   - "Free shipping on orders over $X"

3. **Add Website Link to Stories:**
   - Use Instagram's "Link" sticker
   - Drive traffic from stories to your site

4. **Create Content About Products:**
   - Show products in use (cocktails, charcuterie boards)
   - Link to specific product pages
   - Share customer photos

**Result:** Your existing followers will visit your website!

**Free ways to get backlinks:**

2. **Product Directories:**
