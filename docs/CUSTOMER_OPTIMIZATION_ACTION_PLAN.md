# Customer Optimization Action Plan for Pure Peel Co.

## 🎯 Your Goal: Make the Website Most Optimal for Customers

This is your step-by-step action plan to maximize customer experience and conversions.

---

## 📋 Phase 1: Quick Wins (This Week) - Highest ROI

### 1. FAQ Section ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 1-2 hours  
**Impact:** Reduces support by 30-50%, builds trust, improves SEO

**What to do:**
- Create `/faq` page
- Add common questions about:
  - Product storage and shelf life
  - Size differences
  - Usage in cocktails/recipes
  - Shipping times
  - Returns process
- Add FAQ link to footer and navigation

**Expected Result:** Fewer support emails, more confident customers

---

### 2. Email Newsletter Signup ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 2-3 hours  
**Impact:** Builds customer list, repeat sales, abandoned cart recovery

**What to do:**
- Add newsletter signup to footer
- Create exit-intent popup: "Get 10% off your first order!"
- Integrate with Resend (you already have it set up)
- Send welcome email with discount code
- Store emails in database or Resend audience

**Expected Result:** Direct marketing channel, 10-15% more repeat customers

---

### 3. Trust Badges ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 30 minutes  
**Impact:** Builds confidence, reduces hesitation

**What to do:**
- Add badges to homepage and product pages:
  - "100% Natural"
  - "Made in Canada"
  - "No Preservatives"
  - "Secure Checkout"
- Add customer count: "Join 1,000+ happy customers"

**Expected Result:** More trust = more conversions

---

## 📋 Phase 2: High Impact (Next 2 Weeks)

### 4. Customer Reviews & Ratings ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 4-6 hours  
**Impact:** +15-30% conversion rate

**What to do:**
- Add review section to product pages
- Create review form (send after order delivery)
- Display star ratings
- Show "Verified Purchase" badges
- Store reviews in database or use service (Trustpilot, Yotpo)

**Expected Result:** Social proof = significantly more sales

---

### 5. Product Recommendations ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 3-4 hours  
**Impact:** +20-30% average order value

**What to do:**
- Add "You may also like" section on product pages
- Track recently viewed products
- Create "Frequently bought together" suggestions
- Add bundle deals: "Buy 3, Get 10% off"

**Expected Result:** Customers buy more items per order

---

### 6. Live Chat Widget ⭐⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 1 hour (setup)  
**Impact:** Reduces cart abandonment, answers questions quickly

**What to do:**
- Sign up for free Tawk.to account
- Add chat widget to website
- Set up automated responses
- Forward to email when offline

**Expected Result:** More sales, better customer service

---

## 📋 Phase 3: Enhancements (Next Month)

### 7. Blog / Recipe Section ⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** Ongoing (content creation)  
**Impact:** SEO, content marketing, showcases product uses

**What to do:**
- Create blog section
- Write recipe posts:
  - "10 Cocktail Recipes Using Dehydrated Citrus"
  - "Perfect Charcuterie Board Guide"
  - "How to Use Dehydrated Citrus in Baking"
- Add to navigation

**Expected Result:** More organic traffic, showcases product uses

---

### 8. Wishlist / Favorites ⭐⭐⭐
**Time:** 3-4 hours  
**Impact:** Encourages return visits

**What to do:**
- Add "Add to Wishlist" button
- Create wishlist page
- Store in localStorage
- Email reminders for wishlist items

**Expected Result:** Customers return to complete purchases
