## SEO Strategies (Free, Long-term)

### 5. Optimize Product Pages ⭐⭐⭐⭐

**Make each product page better:**

**Check each page:**
- `/orange`
- `/pink-orange`
- `/lime`
- `/lemon`
- `/apple`

**What to improve:**
- ✅ Longer descriptions (200-300 words)
- ✅ Include keywords naturally
- ✅ Add usage ideas
- ✅ Include benefits
- ✅ Add customer reviews (if possible)
- ✅ Optimize images (alt text, file names)

**Example:**
Instead of: "Crisp, sweet, and aromatic."
Use: "Our premium dehydrated orange slices are crisp, sweet, and aromatic. Made in Canada with 100% natural ingredients, these dried citrus slices are perfect for elevating your cocktails, adding flavor to teas, or creating beautiful charcuterie board presentations. Each slice is carefully dehydrated to preserve natural flavors and vibrant color, making them ideal for both culinary use and decorative purposes."

**Result:** Better rankings in Google, more organic traffic!

## Paid Advertising (Faster Results, Costs Money)

### 8. Google Ads ⭐⭐⭐

**Show up at top of search results:**

**How it works:**
- Pay per click (only pay when someone clicks)
- Target keywords like "dehydrated citrus Canada"
- Set daily budget (start with $10-20/day)

**Benefits:**
- Immediate results
- Target specific keywords
- Control your budget

**Cost:** $300-600/month for small campaigns

**Result:** Immediate traffic from Google search!

---

### 9. Facebook/Instagram Ads ⭐⭐⭐

**Target people interested in your products:**

**How it works:**
- Target by interests (food, cocktails, gourmet)
- Target by location (Canada)
- Show ads to your followers
- Set daily budget

**Benefits:**
- Visual ads (great for food products)
- Target specific audiences
- Drive traffic to website

**Cost:** $200-500/month for small campaigns

**Result:** Traffic from social media!

---

### 10. Influencer Partnerships ⭐⭐⭐

**Partner with food bloggers/influencers:**

**How it works:**
- Send free products to influencers
- They review/feature your products
- Link to your website
- Exposure to their audience

**Who to reach out to:**
- Food bloggers (10K-100K followers)
- Cocktail recipe accounts
- Canadian food influencers
- Charcuterie board accounts

**Cost:** Free products (or paid partnerships)

**Result:** Traffic from influencer audiences!
