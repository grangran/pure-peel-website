## Immediate Actions in Google Search Console

### Step 1: Request Indexing for Homepage

1. **Go to Google Search Console:**
   - https://search.google.com/search-console
   - Select property: `https://purepeelco.com/`

2. **Request Indexing:**
   - Click **"URL Inspection"** in left sidebar
   - Enter: `https://purepeelco.com`
   - Click **"Test Live URL"**
   - Click **"Request Indexing"**

### Step 2: Submit Updated Sitemap

1. **In Google Search Console:**
   - Go to **"Sitemaps"** (under Indexing)
   - Resubmit `sitemap.xml`
   - This tells Google to re-crawl with new keywords

### Step 3: Check Coverage Report

1. **Go to "Coverage"** (under Indexing)
2. **Check for errors:**
   - Make sure homepage is indexed
   - Check for any crawl errors
